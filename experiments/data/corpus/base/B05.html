Care for everyone in our city

CityCare runs five public health clinics across the city. Our nurses, doctors and pharmacists see more than two thousand people every week, and every appointment is free at the point of use.

Book an appointment

Most clinics are open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00 and on Saturday morning from 9:00 to 13:00.

Our services

  • Vaccinations

    Flu, COVID-19 and travel vaccinations for adults and children, with evening sessions at Riverside.

  • Check-ups

    Free health checks for adults aged 40 to 74, including blood pressure and cholesterol tests.

  • Pharmacy

    Collect prescriptions and get advice on minor illnesses from our pharmacists, without an appointment.
