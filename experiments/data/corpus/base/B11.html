How to prepare for a blood test

Most blood tests need no preparation, but some ask you to stop eating for a few hours first. This page explains what to do before, during and after your test.

Before your test

Check your appointment letter. If it says "fasting", do not eat or drink anything except water for 8 to 12 hours before the test. Keep taking your usual medicines unless your doctor tells you otherwise.

Drinking water

Drinking water before the test makes it easier for the nurse to find a vein.

On the day

Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily. Tell the nurse if you feel faint when giving blood, so you can lie down during the test.

After your test

You can go back to your normal activities straight away. Results usually take three to five working days and appear in your CityCare account.
