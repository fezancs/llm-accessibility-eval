Welcome to CityCare

CityCare is a network of public health clinics serving every neighbourhood of the city. Our doctors, nurses and pharmacists offer care for people of all ages, whatever their background.

You can visit any of our clinics for check-ups, vaccinations, prescriptions and advice. Most services are free at the point of use, and our staff are happy to help you find the right care.
