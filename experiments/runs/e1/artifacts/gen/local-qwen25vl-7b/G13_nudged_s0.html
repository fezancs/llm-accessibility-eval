Before your test

Follow these steps to ensure a smooth blood test experience: Avoid eating or drinking for 8-12 hours before the test, wear comfortable clothing, and inform your doctor about any medications you're taking.

On the day

Arrive at the test location on time. Bring any necessary documents and a list of medications. Be prepared to answer questions about your health history.

After your test

After the test, you may experience mild discomfort or bruising. Drink plenty of water and rest. If you have any concerns, contact your healthcare provider.
