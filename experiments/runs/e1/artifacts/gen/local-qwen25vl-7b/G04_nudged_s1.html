How do I book an appointment?
To book an appointment, please visit our website or call our customer service at 123-456-7890. You can also stop by our office at 123 Main Street, Suite 100.
Can I bring someone with me?
Yes, you are welcome to bring a friend or family member with you to your appointment. They can provide support and help you understand the information provided.
Is parking available?
Parking is available in our lot, which is located behind our office. You can park there and walk to the building.
How do I get my test results?
Your test results will be available within 24 hours after your appointment. You can access them online through our secure patient portal or by calling our office at 123-456-7890.
What if I need an interpreter?
If you need an interpreter, please let us know when you book your appointment. We will arrange for a certified interpreter to be present during your visit.
