How to prepare for a blood test

Before your test

Follow these steps to ensure a smooth blood test experience: Avoid eating or drinking for 8-12 hours before the test, wear comfortable clothing, and inform your doctor about any medications you're taking.

On the day

Arrive at the testing location on time. Have your identification ready. The nurse will ask you to sign a consent form and may ask you to provide a small blood sample from a finger prick.

After your test

After the test, you can resume your normal activities. Drink plenty of water and eat a light meal. If you feel faint, sit or lie down and rest.
