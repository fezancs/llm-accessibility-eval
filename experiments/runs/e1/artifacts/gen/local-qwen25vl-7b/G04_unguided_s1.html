How do I book an appointment?
To book an appointment, please call our office at 555-1234 or visit our website. Appointments are available on weekdays from 9 AM to 5 PM.
Can I bring someone with me?
Yes, you are welcome to bring a friend or family member with you to your appointment. Just let us know when you book your appointment.
Is parking available?
Parking is available in our lot, which is located behind the building. Please park there and walk to the entrance.
How do I get my test results?
Your test results will be available in your electronic health record. You can access it through our website or by calling our office. If you prefer, we can also send the results to you by mail.
What if I need an interpreter?
If you need an interpreter, please let us know when you book your appointment. We will arrange for a certified interpreter to be present during your visit.
