How to prepare for a blood test

Before your test

Follow these steps to ensure a smooth blood test experience: Avoid eating or drinking for 8-12 hours before the test, wear comfortable clothing, and inform your doctor about any medications you're taking.

On the day

Arrive at the test location on time. Bring any necessary documents, such as your ID and insurance card. Follow the instructions given by the medical professional administering the test.

After your test

After the test, you may experience some discomfort or bruising at the site. Drink plenty of water and rest. If you have any concerns, contact your healthcare provider.
