How to Prepare for a Blood Test

Home > Health information > Tests

Before your test

Before your blood test, inform your healthcare provider about any medications, supplements, or allergies. Fast for 8-12 hours unless instructed otherwise. Avoid alcohol and caffeine. Wear comfortable clothing. Bring your ID and insurance information. Prepare questions for your doctor.

On the day

On the day of your test, arrive early to complete paperwork. Avoid tight clothing to ease vein access. Stay relaxed, as tension can make veins harder to find. Follow any specific instructions from your healthcare provider. If fasting, do not eat or drink anything except water.

After your test

After your test, apply pressure to the puncture site to prevent bleeding. Monitor for bruising or swelling. Keep the area clean. Avoid strenuous activity for a few hours. Results will be sent to your healthcare provider, who will discuss them with you.
