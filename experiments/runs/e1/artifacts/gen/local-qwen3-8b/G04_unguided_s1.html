How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by visiting our website or calling us. Please select a date and time that suits your schedule.
Can I bring someone with me?
Yes, you can bring a friend or family member. They will need to wait in the waiting area.
Is parking available?
Yes, there is a parking lot available for visitors. It is located across the street from the building.
How do I get my test results?
Your test results will be sent to you via email. You can also check them online through your account.
What if I need an interpreter?
We can provide an interpreter upon request. Please notify us in advance so we can arrange this for you.
