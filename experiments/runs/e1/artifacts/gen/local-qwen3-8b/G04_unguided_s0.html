How do I book an appointment?
To book an appointment, visit our website and select the "Book Now" option. You can also call our office during business hours.
Can I bring someone with me?
Yes, you can bring a companion or family member. Please inform us in advance if you need assistance.
Is parking available?
Parking is available in the lot behind the building. A map with directions will be provided upon arrival.
How do I get my test results?
Test results are typically available online within 5 business days. You'll receive an email notification when they're ready.
What if I need an interpreter?
We provide free interpreter services. Please notify us at least 48 hours in advance to arrange this.
