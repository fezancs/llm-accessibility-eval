How do I book an appointment?

Visit our website and select the "Book Now" button. Choose your preferred date and time.

If you need assistance, contact our team at support@example.com.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes, you may bring one guest with you for support.

Please inform us in advance if you'll have a guest to ensure seating arrangements.

Is parking available?

Yes, there is a parking lot adjacent to the building.

Validation is available for visitors, and spaces are limited.

How do I get my test results?

Your results will be sent to the email address provided during booking.

Results are typically available within 5 business days.

What if I need an interpreter?

Interpreters are available upon request for all appointments.

Notify us at least 48 hours in advance to arrange this service.
