How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment through our website or by calling our office. Please select a date and time that suits your schedule.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes, you can bring a support person or family member with you. We recommend informing us in advance so we can accommodate your needs.

Is parking available?

Yes, limited parking is available on-site. Please check our website for details on parking locations and hours.

How do I get my test results?

You can access your test results through your patient portal or by requesting a copy via email. Results are typically available within 5 business days.

What if I need an interpreter?

We can provide an interpreter in over 100 languages. Please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to arrange this service.
