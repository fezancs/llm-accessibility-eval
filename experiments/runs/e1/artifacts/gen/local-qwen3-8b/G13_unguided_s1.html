How to Prepare for a Blood Test

Before your test

Prepare by fasting for 8-12 hours before the test, avoiding food and drinks except water. Inform your healthcare provider about medications, supplements, or allergies. Stay hydrated and avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior. Bring your ID and insurance information. Some tests may require a specific schedule, so confirm with your doctor.

On the day

Wear comfortable clothing with easy access to your arm. Avoid tight clothing or jewelry on the test site. Bring any required documents and a list of current medications. The procedure involves a small needle prick to draw blood, which is quick and generally painless. Remain still during the process to ensure accuracy.

After your test

Rest for a few minutes and apply pressure to the site if needed. Results are usually available within 1-3 days. Follow your doctor’s advice on diet or activity. If you feel unwell after the test, contact your healthcare provider. Keep the results secure and share them with your doctor as instructed.
