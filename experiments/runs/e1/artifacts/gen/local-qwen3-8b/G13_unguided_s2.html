How to prepare for a blood test

Before your test

Prepare by fasting for 8-12 hours before the test, avoiding food and drinks except water. Inform your doctor about medications, supplements, or allergies. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours. Bring your ID, insurance card, and any requested forms. Discuss any concerns with your healthcare provider beforehand.

On the day

Arrive early to complete paperwork and answer questions. Wear a short-sleeve shirt to ease access to your arm. Avoid smoking and caffeine before the test. Stay hydrated but don't eat. Remain still during the procedure to minimize discomfort. Follow staff instructions carefully.

After your test

Rest for a few minutes and check for bruising at the site. Results may take 1-3 days; contact your doctor for interpretations. Avoid heavy lifting or strenuous activity for 24 hours. Resume normal activities unless advised otherwise. Keep the site clean and monitor for infection signs.
